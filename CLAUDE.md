## Writing style

- **Em dashes:** Avoid. Use commas, semicolons, or parentheses instead.
- **"Genuine" and derivatives** (genuinely, genuineness, etc.): Avoid unless the context requires an explicit contrast with a non-genuine version mentioned nearby. The word is almost always filler.
- **Sentence length:** Use short sentences for the point; longer sentences for elaboration. Vary deliberately — the contrast does work.
- **Repetition:** Repeat a word when it is the right word. Do not hunt for synonyms to avoid repetition.
- **Closing sentences:** When a passage earns it, end with a short crystallized sentence that states the conclusion plainly.
